How the season goes

  • Mid-June
    Strawberries
    About three weeks. Come early.
  • Early July
    Raspberries
    Right as strawberries wind down.
  • August
    Wild plums
    By the quart, and as jam.
  • September
    Fall add-on
    Squash, apples, onions.
  • October
    Pumpkins
    A small patch, weekends only.

About the farm

Three generations have worked this ground, and the wild plum thicket by the road was here before any of us. We grow what this land is good at and sell it straight to the families who eat it.
