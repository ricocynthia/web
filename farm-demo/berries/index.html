Hi, we're the Vangs.

Mai's parents planted our first strawberry rows thirty years ago. The wild plum thicket by the road was here long before any of us, so the farm got its name.

We grow what this ground is good at and sell it straight to the families who eat it. Come pick a quart and say hi.

Mai and Tom Vang
